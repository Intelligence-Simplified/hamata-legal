Disconnect & delete local data

Hamata does not keep a separate Hamata account. Here is how to remove the app’s access and wipe its on-device index.

In the app (preferred)

  1. Open Hamata on your Android device.
  2. Open the overflow menu (⋮) on the home screen.
  3. Choose Disconnect (or equivalent account disconnect).
  4. Confirm. Hamata removes the Google session from the app and deletes its local header and body cache for that account on this device.

If you already uninstalled Hamata

  • Uninstalling removes Hamata’s local storage on that device.
  • Also revoke app access in your Google Account: Google Account → Security → Third-party access / Your connections to third-party apps → remove Hamata if listed.

Request help by email

If you cannot use the in-app path, email hamata@intelligencesimplified.com with the Google account email you used in Hamata. We will confirm how to revoke Google access and that we do not hold a copy of your mail on our servers (Hamata has no mail backend).

What this does not do

  • It does not delete your Gmail messages at Google.
  • It does not close your Google Account.
  • Mail you archived or trashed from Hamata already changed labels in Gmail; manage those in Gmail if you want them restored or purged there.
