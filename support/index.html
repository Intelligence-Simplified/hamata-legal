How to get help

  • Describe what you were trying to do, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Mention your Android version and device model if the issue involves sign-in, sync, or bulk actions.
  • For Google sign-in problems, confirm you are using a Google account that is allowed to access the app (during Testing, only listed test users can sign in) and that Gmail access was granted.
  • For sync progress that seems stuck, keep the app open or reopen it so header download can resume; large mailboxes can take a long time on first sync.
  • To leave Hamata and clear its local copy of your headers, use Disconnect in the app or see Disconnect & delete local data.
